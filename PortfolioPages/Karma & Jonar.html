Karma & Jonar (Unreal Engine 5, Blueprint, C++)

Project information

  • Category: Unreal Engine 5, Blueprint, C++
  • Motive: Multiplayer Co-op Gameplay Experience
  • Project date: 2024
  • Project Trailer: Video

Developed as part of a game project inspired by the animated series from Viaplay Studios, Karma & Jonar is a 2-player split-screen cooperative game designed to deliver a seamless and engaging multiplayer experience. This project required the integration of multiple advanced gameplay systems and AI behaviors to create dynamic, replayable encounters.

  1. Designed and implemented a split-screen multiplayer system with synchronized camera controls and smooth player interactions for local co-op gameplay.
  2. Developed an advanced locomotion and distance matching system to ensure realistic character movement and fluid transitions between animations.
  3. Implemented a two-player interaction system, allowing players to work together in solving challenges, triggering in-game events, and performing joint actions.
  4. Created an intelligent Rock Troll AI capable of adaptive combat strategies, environmental navigation, and reactive animations, making encounters more dynamic and challenging.
  5. Designed a Bow Weapon System with precise aiming mechanics, realistic projectile physics, and responsive feedback for immersive ranged combat.
  6. Collaborated closely with the team to balance gameplay mechanics, refine level design, and ensure a polished cooperative experience.

This project emphasized teamwork, real-time player coordination, and cinematic gameplay moments, blending technical implementation with creative design. Through extensive playtesting and iteration, the result was an immersive cooperative adventure that reflects the charm and spirit of the original animated series.
